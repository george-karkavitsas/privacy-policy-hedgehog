Master Password

To enter and use the Service, you must create a “Master Password,” which is used to generate the encryption key that secures the information you store in the Apps (“Secured Data” as further defined below). This is because, even if a hacker somehow obtained all the Secured Data on our servers, they would have to hack each Account separately (because Secured Data is always encrypted when we have it, and the encryption is based on the Account’s Master Password). Each user must create their own Master Password. Our Zero Knowledge technology ensures that we do not and cannot know our users’ Master Passwords or the data used to generate the encryption key, so we cannot access Secured Data.

Forgot Master Password

The Master Password is used to generate the encryption key that encrypts your data. You are responsible for keeping your Master Password safe. The app deletes your local entries after the configured number of incorrect Master Password attempts. This does not delete your cloud backup, but restoring it requires the Master Password used to encrypt it.

Secured Data

Our Apps let you manage digital identity data, including highly sensitive information like credit card numbers and site or application credentials. This, and everything else you store on the Service, is Secured Data, and is encrypted and stored locally on your device(s) and on our servers using a random key generated from your Master Password and your Google account mail. Secured Data is encrypted at all times on our servers and cannot be accessed by us because the encryption key is generated from the Master Password and your Google account mail. While We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute security.
