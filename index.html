As used in this Policy, “Personal Data” means information which, either alone or when combined with other information we hold, identifies an individual, such as name, mailing address, email address, IP address, and telephone number. By contrast, “Anonymous Data” means data that, alone or combined with other information available to us or a third party with whom the data is shared, does not permit identification of an individual. We collect and use both Personal Data and Anonymous Data as described below.

Master Password

To enter and use the Service, you must create a “Master Password,” which is used to generate the encryption key that secures the information you store in the Apps (“Secured Data” as further defined below). This is because, even if a hacker somehow obtained all the Secured Data on our servers, they would have to hack each Account separately (because Secured Data is always encrypted when we have it, and the encryption is based on the Account’s Master Password). Each user must create their own Master Password. For manual unlock, HedgeHog stores an encrypted record on your device to verify the Master Password you enter. If you set up fingerprint or face unlock, HedgeHog also stores an encrypted copy of your Master Password on that device. Android Keystore holds the key for that copy and requires biometric approval to use it. Both records use AES-256 encryption and stay on your device. Our Zero Knowledge technology ensures that we do not and cannot know our users’ Master Passwords, so we cannot access Secured Data.

Forgot Master Password

The Master Password is used to generate the encryption key that encrypts your data. You are responsible for keeping your Master Password safe. The app deletes your local entries after the configured number of incorrect Master Password attempts. This does not delete your cloud backup, but restoring it requires the Master Password used to encrypt it.

Secured Data

Our app lets you manage digital identity data, including highly sensitive information like credit card numbers and site or application credentials. Usernames, email addresses, passwords, notes, PINs, card security codes, seed phrases and wallet keys are encrypted on your device. Entry titles, website links, entry types, favourite status, expiration dates, identifiers, timestamps and certain other metadata are not encrypted by the app. If you choose cloud backup, the protected fields remain encrypted in the backup, and the metadata is also included. While We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Security

We use Google Firebase services to support the Service, including Cloud Storage for Firebase for cloud backups. According to Google's published documentation, Firebase protects data in transit using HTTPS, and Cloud Storage for Firebase encrypts stored data at rest. These infrastructure protections are separate from the app's encryption of protected entry fields, described in the “Secured Data” section. Further information about Firebase's security practices is available at Privacy and Security in Firebase. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
