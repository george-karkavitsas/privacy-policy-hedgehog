Your master password and local vault

Your master password helps encrypt your vault. Keep it confidential and somewhere you can retrieve it securely. GVK Software cannot recover your master password or decrypt your protected vault data for you. Restoring a cloud backup requires the same master password used to encrypt it.

HedgeHog deletes the local vault when the configured limit for incorrect master password attempts is reached. Clearing your master password in Settings also deletes the local vault. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Before resetting or replacing a device, check that you have access to any backup you intend to restore.

Premium cloud features

Premium includes saving, restoring and syncing vault data through Google Firebase. Cloud features require an internet connection and Google sign-in. To restore a backup, sign in with the Google account used to save it. You can delete a cloud backup using Delete data from server in the app when the backup is available.

HedgeHog encrypts sensitive vault fields. Some metadata, including entry titles and website links, is stored without app-level encryption and is included in cloud backups. Our HedgeHog Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data.
